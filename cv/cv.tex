\section{Research} My research develops models for understanding and improving the \textit{resilience} of modern socio-technical systems with downstream applications to platforms, supply chains, and organizations. I study the mechanisms causing fragility and devise analytical metrics to measure the resilience, prescriptive interventions, and decentralized decision-making under uncertainty and information constraints.
